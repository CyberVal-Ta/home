Past Experiences And My Future

From my multiple experiences with Computing, a foundation for the future I want to create was made. My early interest in robotics in Grade 6 sparked curiosity about how technology interacts with the real world. Through Grades 7–10, experimenting with games and animations taught me the importance of combining creativity and logic in problem-solving. In Grades 11–12, working with Information Processes and Technology helped me realise how structured systems and web development could be applied to real-world projects.

A unique influence during my later high school years was my fascination with cyberpunk fashion and themes. What started as an aesthetic interest grew into something deeper, the idea of technology in dystopian or futuristic contexts made me curious about the vulnerabilities of digital systems and the role of cybersecurity in protecting society. This interest helped me link my passion for computing with a clear academic and career focus.

Then my first year of university started and these experiences came together where I gained practical skills in programming, networking, and cybersecurity. Each step reinforced the idea that computing is more than just code—it is a way to solve problems,create meaningful tools, and protect systems in an increasingly digital world.

All these experiences have given me technical skills alongside a goal to channel all these experiences into cybersecurity and ethical hacking as these influences solidified as I gained hands-on experience with coding, networking and cybersecurity concepts
