My Computing History

During primary school, I discovered my interest in computers through robotics. Then, through high school, I explored basic software and experimented with simple programming projects. Over the years, I have studied subjects like Information Technology and Software Design, which introduced me to coding, web development and structured problem-solving.

Grade 6

I was first introduced to robotics. Building and programming simple robots gave me an early sense of how code could interact with the physical world, which sparked my curiosity about technology and engineering. Aside from this, I was also introduced to gaming, which also piqued my interest.

Grade 7

During highschool, I began spending more time in the computer labs, where I experimented with different platforms to create basic games. This was my first experience with combining creativity and logic in computing.

Grade 8 - 10

I explored animations and more advanced game development, learning how to bring movement and interaction into digital projects. A highlight was a school trip where I built a small programmable device that could display words on a screen, this was a hands-on experience that showed me how software connects with hardware.

Grade 11 - 12

My focus honestly shifted to Information Processes and Technology (IPT). I learned how to design and build websites, manage information systems, and complete a variety of small projects. These experiences strengthened my understanding of computing as both a creative tool.

Lowkey, I became interested in Cyberpunk fashion and themes during this time. This creative curiosity led me to explore the concept of cybersecurity within a dystopian world. What started as an aesthetic interest soon became an academic focus and ultimately, one of the main areas I wanted to study in depth.

First Uni Year

I entered a much deeper stage of learning, working on more technical and complex projects. I created a web-based game called Clara the Ladybug, built websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Django, and gained experience with Python, Java, and SQL. I also learned to use Linux systems, explored computer networking, and began to understand how different parts of computing fit together in real-world applications. While this wasn't an easy process, I learnt a lot, which only continued to fuel my interest in Cybersecurity and Computing.
